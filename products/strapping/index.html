Overview

Polypropylene strapping bundles cartons, sheets and pallets securely and is much lighter and safer to handle than steel strap. We supply rolls in different widths and colours, plus the clips and sealers to fasten them.

Tell us your load and strapping method (machine, semi-automatic or hand tool) and we will recommend the width and thickness.

Specifications

Material
Polypropylene (PP) strap
Widths
9 mm, 12 mm, 15 mm, 19 mm
Thickness
About 0.5 – 1.0 mm (0.6 – 0.8 mm typical)
Colours
White, black, red, blue, green and others
Roll length
Typically 1000 – 2000 m
Break strength
Roughly 80 – 190 kg depending on width and thickness — confirm for your load
Grades
Machine grade, semi-automatic grade
Accessories
Metal / plastic clips, sealers, tensioners, edge guards

Which width to use

WidthTypical use
9 mmLight cartons and small bundles
12 mmGeneral carton strapping (machine / semi-auto)
15 mmHeavier cartons and bundles
19 mmHeavy bundles and pallet strapping

Exact break strength and roll weight vary by make — ask us for the current stock.

Common questions

Do you supply the clips and tools too?

Clips and sealers are available. Tell us your strapping method and we will suggest the right set.

PP or PET strap?

PP is economical for carton and bundle strapping. For heavier loads ask us about stronger options.
