FAQ

Common questions

What thicknesses and sizes of PVC sheet do you supply?

Rigid grey PVC sheets are commonly available from 1 mm to 20 mm in a standard 1220 × 2440 mm size. Other thicknesses, colours and cut sizes can be arranged. Send us your requirement.

Is PVC sheet suitable for electrical insulation?

Rigid PVC is an electrical insulator and is widely used for insulation panels, barriers and meter boxes. For safety-critical use, tell us your voltage, flame rating and standard (IS / IEC) and we will confirm the right grade and share the test certificate.

What is the minimum order quantity?

We serve both small and bulk buyers. Wholesale rates apply from bulk quantities. Message us your requirement and we will share the best price.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We dispatch by road transport across India. Freight is quoted along with the material, and you receive a GST invoice and LR copy.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes, samples of PVC sheet and packaging material can be arranged on request. Courier charges may apply.

How do I get a quote quickly?

The fastest way is WhatsApp. Send the product, thickness or size, quantity and delivery city. You can also use the form below or email us.

What are your payment terms?

New buyers pay in advance (UPI, NEFT / RTGS or bank transfer). Regular buyers can request credit terms, which are offered after approval.

How long does delivery take?

Delhi-NCR orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 working days. For other cities, transit takes about 3–7 days depending on the route. Cut-to-size orders may need an extra day.

Can you print my logo on BOPP tape?

Yes, custom-printed BOPP tape with your brand name or logo is available in bulk quantities. Share your artwork and we will confirm the minimum quantity and lead time.

Can you cut PVC sheets to my drawing or size?

Yes. Send us your drawing or dimensions and we will cut the sheets to size, so you save on wastage and fabrication time.
